# User Interviews

Goal: at least 3 people, in person or on a call. Ask about their experience
("Tell me about the last time you..."). Don't describe the app until the end.
Write down what they said **in their words**.

## Questions — family members who want to recreate dishes (cousins, aunt, me)

1. Tell me about the last time you tried to make a dish that someone in the family
   makes. What happened?
2. When you want to know how Grandma makes something, what do you do? Walk me
   through the last time.
3. Tell me about the last time a recipe didn't turn out the way you expected. What
   did you do next?
4. When you're cooking and a step doesn't make sense, or you want it to taste
   different, what do you do in that moment?
5. Tell me about the last time you saw a dish or technique you wanted to try later.
   What did you do with it? Did you ever come back to it?

## Questions — Grandma (the recipe holder)

1. Tell me about the last time someone asked you how to make one of your dishes.
   What did you tell them?
2. How did you learn to make your yogurt? Has the way you make it changed?
3. When you make flan, how do you know it's ready?
4. Have you ever written a recipe down, or had someone write one down for you?
   What happened to it?
5. What do you use your phone for during a normal day?
